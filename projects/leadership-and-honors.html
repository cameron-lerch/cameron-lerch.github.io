Graduate Engineering Community Organization (GECO), Yale

Activities Coordinator, TGIF Manager & Project Manager · 2022 – 2025

GECO was started in 2022 by my good friend Ethen Lund, at first to put together a dedicated lounge for engineering graduate students. It soon became much more. We hosted events for the entire School of Engineering & Applied Science (SEAS), across all five departments, to build community and a sense of belonging for engineering students at Yale. Events ranged from monthly TGIFs with catered food, drinks, and outdoor games to bake-offs and the annual SEAS tailgate for The Game (Harvard vs. Yale).

  • Was there from the start: helped pick out the furniture for the Mann Engineering Student Lounge and set up the space.
  • Helped run our monthly TGIF events.
  • Co-hosted the annual Paint-a-Pot and Plant-a-Plant event: picked out house plants and pots for students to decorate, then taught them how to pot their new plant and care for it.
  • Hosted Nintendo Switch game nights and board game nights, with snacks, in the student lounge.
  • Co-hosted the annual SEAS tailgate for The Game.
  • Helped the other activities coordinators with their events.

Missouri S&T Astronomical Research Society (STARS)

Co-founder & Vice President · September 2016 – May 2018

I helped found and run Missouri S&T's Astronomical Research Society, a group of astronomy-loving students from across campus. Members met once a week, taking turns presenting something related to astronomy. When the weather was right we hosted viewing events at the on-campus observatory, which drew a mix of students and families from the local community.

  • Communicated with members each week about meeting times, events, and announcements.
  • Coordinated community outreach events at Missouri S&T's observatory.
  • Managed the society's equipment, including the telescope and astronomy textbooks.
  • Kept the society's Facebook and other social media pages up to date.
