Electrolysis Stack Enclosure

An airtight, mobile enclosure for a large-scale electrolysis stack, built to OSHA standards to keep people safe around carbon monoxide.

Oxylus Energy

The finished enclosure with the electrolysis stack inside (left) and the Autodesk Inventor model (right).

Overview

I designed and fabricated an airtight enclosure for Oxylus Energy's large-scale electrolysis stack. It was built to OSHA standards so that the chamber met every requirement for safely operating with and handling carbon monoxide gas.

Requirements

  • Support the 175 lb stack and its hardware (I designed it to carry 500 lb).
  • Roll on wheels, and fit through the lab door so it could move with the company if Oxylus changed locations.
  • Vent into a fume hood.
  • Include a shelf for the experiment's electrical equipment.
  • Keep people safe even in a worst-case carbon monoxide leak inside the chamber.

Design

I designed the enclosure from the ground up in Autodesk Inventor, choosing 80/20 aluminum framing and polycarbonate panels as the primary materials, then fabricated and assembled it.

My role

Requirements, CAD design, ventilation and safety analysis, and fabrication.
