Overview

After switching to Prof. Ian Abraham's lab, I researched robot control and trajectory planning for safe autonomous search and exploration. In late 2022 I passed my qualifying exam, presented my thesis prospectus to my committee, and was admitted to Ph.D. candidacy.

  • Developed novel control algorithms for autonomous search and exploration with multi-robot systems.
  • Used optimization-based trajectory planning for multi-robot teams.
  • Engineered safety-critical control frameworks using control barrier functions for safe search and exploration in cluttered environments.
  • Ran real-world and simulated experiments with teams of drones.
